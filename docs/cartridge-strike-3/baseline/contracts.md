Implement each new field with its first real consumer and a regression test. The [integration matrix](../RIPOSTE_INTEGRATION_MATRIX.md) records its source implementation. ## First playable slice

The first input boundary is the browser event. MobileControls retains each stick's unclamped pointer distance. Riposte's touchSprint uses outer/inner radius ratios of 1.15/0.95 and forward gates of 0.55/0.35. Validate these thresholds on the real CS2 stick before adopting them. Desktop currently uses Shift for sneak. Keep the saved binding and make Rush independently reachable. Capture a trigger tap that starts and ends between render frames on either input device.

The existing app samples held inputs in input() and dispatches to Game.update or NetworkSession.advanceFrame. The latter can produce up to six fixed frames per draw. NetworkRoom.fixedTick coalesces up to twelve SDK frames. Carry movement from the newest sample independently of any earlier saved shot aim. Server lag compensation validates the original sample when the weapon is ready. Keep the same readiness rule for local prediction, server ammo, one recoil/audio cue and confirmation. Reconciliation replays movement only.

The long-gun local pose in 06-weapons VM_SPRINT is XYZ (+0.01, −0.04, −0.04) m and rotation (−0.5, +0.65, +0.7) rad. updateViewmodel uses stiffness 270 and damping 18. The pistol uses 06c-models pistolRunPose with a free support hand. Port the reference silhouette through the CS2 rig axes and verify muzzle, grip and reticle on screen. The donor's controller damps at 11/s and gates fire below a 0.3 sprint weight. Starting at one gives about seven 60 Hz steps of recovery. Measure the CS3 result against V4 and both normal-entry baselines before locking tuning.
