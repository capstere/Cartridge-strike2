Activation takes an exclusive operator lock. Once the Pi reports zero connected and reserved rooms, the manager briefly freezes the dedicated public connector, checks the authority twice, records the previous/candidate revisions in a durable private cutover record and switches the service. It reopens the connector before the public WSS proof. If that proof fails after a player enters, rollback waits for the match and its reconnect reservation to drain. A separate systemd guard thaws the connector after manager loss. A failed or interrupted operation is resumable from the pinned manager using `resume`; it verifies the previous service with two independent public clients. If authority occupancy is unknown, inspect the service before resuming. The probe drops to the game UID. The offline rollback converter runs under that UID on disposable copies and publishes only a validated family envelope and restorable database. The original account database and journal remain private. The retained backup shares the Pi disk. Off-device disaster recovery remains untested.
